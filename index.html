01 The Problem

Your content is your storefront.

Before anyone calls you, books you or buys from you, they scroll you. Your Instagram, your website and your ads are where a stranger decides if you are worth their time and their money. That decision takes seconds.

Most businesses lose it right there. The photos are dated. The videos look like everyone else's. The feed goes quiet for weeks, then posts three times in one day. None of it says who you are or why you are different, so people keep scrolling to someone who looks more put together, even when that someone is not as good as you.

Chris learned the other side of this firsthand. His first business was a streetwear brand where social media was the entire storefront. Consistent, high quality content did the selling, and it grew to over half a million in sales in under two years. CD Visuals exists to build that same engine for your business.

04 The Brand Package

Why a complete brand package beats building it piece by piece

A logo from one place, a website from another, photos from a friend and social posts from whoever has time. Each piece might be fine on its own. Together they tell your customer that nobody is steering the ship. A comprehensive branding package fixes that by building every piece from the same story, at the same time, under the same creative direction.

Piece by piece

  • Every vendor interprets your brand a little differently
  • Colors, fonts and tone drift from one platform to the next
  • You end up project managing a team of freelancers
  • Launch keeps slipping because one piece is always missing
  • Content gets redone once the identity finally settles

One complete package

  • One story drives every visual and every word
  • Your logo, website, print and socials clearly belong together
  • One creative director owns the whole picture
  • You launch with everything ready on day one
  • New content plugs straight into a system that already works

What a complete package can include

  • Brand story

    The why behind your business, written so customers feel it and remember it.

  • Logo & visual identity

    A mark, palette and type system that make you recognizable anywhere.

  • Website design & launch

    A home base that looks the part and makes it easy to buy, book or call.

  • Launch content

    Photo and video that introduce your brand the moment it goes live.

  • Print materials

    Business cards and print pieces that carry the same look into the real world.

  • Social templates

    Graphics and templates that keep every post on brand, even the ones you make yourself.

  • Personal brand

    For founders who are the face of the business, a presence built around you and your story.

  • Built around you

    Start with what you need now and add the rest as you grow.

The result is a brand that feels established from the very first impression. Customers trust what looks consistent. Consistency builds recognition, recognition builds trust, and trust is what lets a business raise its prices, win the bigger client and grow by referral.

06 From the Founder

Meet Chris Dodd, founder of CD Visuals

A note from Chris

I'm Chris Dodd, founder and creative director of CD Visuals, based in Rochester, New York. My creative roots start on a skateboard. Growing up skating shaped how I see the world, along with the fashion and music that came with it. That hunger to create took me to Los Angeles in 2012, before social media took over, where I worked in acting, modeling and reality TV, on sets with artists like Maroon 5, Machine Gun Kelly and T.I.

Back in New York, I found my true calling behind the lens through my first business, a streetwear ecommerce brand. I grew it to over half a million in sales in under two years with almost no ad spend. Social media was my entire storefront, and consistent, high quality content did the selling. That grind taught me how to capture attention, build trust and turn views into revenue.

I built CD Visuals on those lessons, starting with yearlong campaigns for men's fashion and streetwear brands that drove millions of impressions and real sales. Today my work centers on creative direction, cinematography and full brand development: shaping how a brand looks, feels and tells its story. I also partner with local businesses and organizations here in Rochester, managing their social media and creating content that connects them with their community.

Social media is where I live. I study it, create for it every day, and practice what I preach by growing my own personal brand alongside my clients'. Outside of work, I'm a man of faith, a father of six, and engaged to the love of my life. I'm obsessed with discipline and self improvement, and I bring that same standard to every client. I love what I do, and it shows in the work.

Chris DoddFounder & Creative Director

07 Questions

Questions about hiring CD Visuals

How much does a brand video or photo shoot cost?

Every project is scoped to your goals, so there is no one-size price list. A headshot session and a full commercial are very different jobs. Share what you have in mind through the project form and you will get a clear quote after a short conversation.

What is included in a comprehensive branding package?

A package can include brand storytelling, logo and visual identity, website design and launch, launch photo and video, business cards and print, social media graphics and templates, and personal brand development for founders. It is shaped around where your business is right now, so you pay for what moves you forward. See how a complete brand package works.

Do you only work with fashion and streetwear brands?

No. Chris built his name in men's fashion and streetwear, and that standard carries into every industry. CD Visuals also works with Rochester local businesses and organizations, hospitality, musicians, founders building a personal brand, and couples on their wedding day.

Can you manage our social media every month?

Yes. Full social media management covers strategy, monthly content creation and posting, with Facebook and Instagram ads, Google paid search and SEO available when you are ready to scale. Explore all social media and marketing services.

Do you shoot weddings and drone footage?

Yes to both. Weddings get the same cinematic eye as a brand film, and drone photography and video add the wide, dramatic shots that make a property, an event or a location feel big.

Why hire one studio instead of separate freelancers?

Your customer experiences your brand as one thing. When the same creative director handles your strategy, your shoots and your social, everything matches, nothing gets lost between vendors, and you spend your time running your business instead of managing freelancers.
